*   **Primary [Gov Data]:** 6-3 Decision. **2. Illinois v. Second Amendment Foundation (Decided: July 01, 2026)**
*   **Primary [Gov Data]:** 5-4 Decision. The Court struck down the Illinois state-level ban on semi-automatic rifles (commonly referred to as "assault weapons"), applying the *Bruen* "history and tradition" test. The Court determined these firearms are in common use for lawful purposes.
*   **Narrative Delta / Conflict Analysis:**

    *   [AltMedia] (e.g., *The Reload*, *Ammoland*): Celebrates the ruling as the long-awaited, definitive vindication of the Second Amendment against state-level legislative overreach.

**3. Texas v. Department of Homeland Security (Decided: June 29, 2026)**
*   **Primary [Gov Data]:** 7-2 Decision. Reaffirmed federal supremacy in border enforcement. The Court ruled that state-deployed physical barriers (including razor wire and buoys) and state-directed detentions in federal border zones violate the Supremacy Clause.
*   **Narrative Delta / Conflict Analysis:**

**4. American Chemistry Council v. EPA (Decided: June 26, 2026)**
*   **Primary [Gov Data]:** 6-3 Decision. The Court aggressively applied its post-*Loper Bright* (anti-*Chevron*) precedent, striking down the EPA's recent microplastics emissions mandates. The Court ruled the agency exceeded statutory authorization explicitly granted by Congress in the Clean Water Act.
*   **Narrative Delta / Conflict Analysis:**

    *   [AltMedia] (e.g., *WSJ Editorial Board*, *Libertarian Substacks*): Praises the reassertion of the separation of powers and the curtailing of unelected bureaucratic overreach.

**5. Florida v. Meta Platforms, Inc. (Decided: June 25, 2026)**
